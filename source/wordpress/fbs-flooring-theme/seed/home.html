Useful to know

Your questions, answered.

What services does FBS offer?

FBS offers free home consultations, measuring and advice, and professional fitting in collaboration with fitting specialists. Discuss your new-home, renovation, residential or commercial project with the team.

Is the home consultation free?

Yes. FBS’s home consultation is free. Fitting and any other agreed project work are quoted separately.

Can I ask about fitting flooring I already have?

Yes. Send the exact flooring specification, room details and any installation instructions. FBS will review the fitting requirements and discuss suitability before agreeing the work.

Where do you work?

FBS is based in Coolock, Dublin. Share your town or Eircode so the team can confirm whether they can arrange a consultation or fitting for your location.
